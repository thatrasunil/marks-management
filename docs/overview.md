# Marks Management System Overview

## 🎯 App Idea
The Marks Management System is an end-to-end robust web application explicitly designed for educational institutions to automate and securely manage result processing. It streamlines the lifecycle of student grading from data ingestion to final result publication, transitioning paper-based or disjointed spreadsheet processes into a structured, easily auditable workflow.

## 🚀 Objectives
1. **Eliminate Manual Errors**: By securely mapping anonymous exam codes (`Unique_ID`) to student identities (`Roll_Number`), the system prevents grading bias and ensures 100% accurate result assignment.
2. **Streamlined Workflows**: Separate responsibilities between **Teachers** (evaluators who input and process bulk marks) and **Admins** (moderators who oversee, approve, and globally publish results).
3. **Bulk Ingestion via Excel**: Support quick uploads of hundreds of marks simultaneously through automated Excel extraction, mapping, and validation.
4. **Real-time Result Computation**: Instantly parse standard raw numbers into normalized grades, GPAs, and SGPAs based on configurable administrative rules.
5. **Secure Student Gateway**: Provide a private, Aadhar-verified portal for students to view their finalized, verified transcripts instantly upon Admin publication.

## 🤝 Target Audience
- **Teachers/Evaluators**: Need a fast, bulk way to ingest and compute internal and external marks for their assigned subjects without worrying about calculating final student grades.
- **Administrators/Examination Cell**: Need ultimate oversight to ensure that no grades are published before formal approval, as well as the ability to notify all students simultaneously.
- **Students**: Need a transparent, reliable, and secure portal to check exactly what their final approved grades and SGPAs are.
